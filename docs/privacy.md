# SocialTea Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026

SocialTea does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Nothing you import ever leaves your phone.

## What the app reads
SocialTea reads follower and following lists only from files you choose to import — the official data exports that Instagram, Facebook and TikTok let you download, or CSV/TXT files you provide. These files are read on your device and never uploaded.

## What the app keeps
- By default, imported lists live in your phone's memory only and are erased when you close the app.
- **Remember last snapshot (optional, off by default).** If you turn this on in Settings, the usernames (and display names, when the export includes them) from your latest import are saved on this phone, together with the date of that import and which platform it came from, so next time you can import just your new export and see who unfollowed you. While it is on, the app also keeps your follower and following totals (numbers only) for the trend chart. This file stays on this phone only: it is never uploaded or sent anywhere, it is encrypted while your phone is locked, and it is left out of iCloud and computer backups. Turning the setting off, or using Start over or Clear everything, erases it immediately. Deleting the app also erases it.
- **Save counts for widget, Siri & Spotlight (optional, off by default).** If you turn this on, only your follower and following totals (no names) are saved on this phone so the widget, Siri and Spotlight can show them. Turning it off erases them.
- The app saves a few settings on your device: whether App Lock is on, your theme color, night mode, reminder preference, and whether you have seen the intro. These settings never leave your phone.

## Purchases
SocialTea Pro is offered as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase, all sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment. SocialTea only checks the purchase status that StoreKit keeps on your device and never receives your payment details.

## Network and third parties
SocialTea has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests of its own. When you tap a profile, the link opens in the official app or in Safari, which are governed by those companies' own policies.

## Sharing
If you use Share, you choose where the file goes. SocialTea creates a temporary copy for the Share Sheet and deletes it afterwards.

## Notifications
If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally by iOS. No notification data is sent anywhere.

## Children
SocialTea collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

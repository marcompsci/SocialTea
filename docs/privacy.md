# SocialTea Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026

SocialTea does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the app reads
SocialTea reads follower and following lists only from files you choose to import — the official data exports that Instagram, Facebook and TikTok let you download, or CSV/TXT files you provide. These files are read on your device and never uploaded.

## What the app keeps
- Imported lists live in your phone's memory only and are erased when you close the app.
- The app saves a few settings on your device: whether App Lock is on, your theme color, night mode, reminder preference, and whether you have seen the intro. These settings never leave your phone.

## Purchases
SocialTea Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment. SocialTea only checks the purchase status that StoreKit keeps on your device and never receives your payment details.

## Network and third parties
SocialTea has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests of its own. When you tap a profile, the link opens in the official app or in Safari, which are governed by those companies' own policies.

## Sharing
If you use Share, you choose where the file goes. SocialTea creates a temporary copy for the Share Sheet and deletes it afterwards.

## Notifications
If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally by iOS. No notification data is sent anywhere.

## Children
SocialTea collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

## Contact

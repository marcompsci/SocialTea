# SocialTea

A private follower tracker that works from your own data exports. No logins, no passwords, nothing uploaded.

## Getting your export
Open the **Guide** tab in the app for step-by-step instructions for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

## SocialTea Pro
SocialTea Pro is $7.99/month through your Apple ID. To cancel or change it, open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your iPhone. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open SocialTea → **Settings → Restore purchases**.

## Help
- Numbers look a little different from the app? Exports are a snapshot from the moment you requested them, and deactivated accounts may be left out.
- "Gone quiet" never means a confirmed block — it only shows accounts that vanished from both of your lists.

Still stuck? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/marcompsci/SocialTea/issues).
